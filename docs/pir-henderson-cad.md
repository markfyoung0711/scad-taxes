# Public Information Request — Henderson County Appraisal District

Texas Government Code Chapter 552. Henderson CAD is a separate district from
Smith: Chandler, Athens, Malakoff and Gun Barrel City are Henderson County, and
Smith CAD holds nothing for them.

Why this rather than a crawl: Henderson's property search sits behind
reCAPTCHA Enterprise and rate-limits to a few requests, so it is not a bulk
source. Their search does expose tax years **2017–2027**, four years deeper
than Smith's, so the records plainly exist — they just have to be asked for.

Send to `hendersoncad@hcadtx.org`, or by mail below.

Henderson County Appraisal District

**Re: Request for certified appraisal rolls, tax years 2000–2026**

To the Public Information Officer:

Under the Texas Public Information Act, Chapter 552 of the Government Code, I
request copies of the following public records.

**Records requested.** The certified appraisal roll for each tax year from 2000
through 2026, in whatever machine-readable form the District already holds —
a delimited export, a fixed-width extract, or a native appraisal-software
export. For each year I am seeking the real property roll, the business
personal property roll, and the mineral roll, together with the
per-jurisdiction taxable values and tax rates and the exemption detail, if
those are held as separate files.

The District's public property search offers tax years 2017 through 2027, so
records for at least that span are evidently retained in electronic form. I am
not asking the District to create a new record or to run a new report design —
only to produce records it already holds. Please include any record layout or
data dictionary that accompanies them.

**Format and delivery.** Electronic delivery is preferred and should be the
least costly option: email, a download link, or an FTP/SFTP transfer. If volume
makes that impractical, I will accept physical media and pay for the media.

**Partial production.** If the District holds some years but not all, please
produce the years it holds rather than treating the request as unfillable, and
tell me which years are unavailable.

**Confidential information.** I am not requesting information the Act excepts
from disclosure — for example addresses confidential under § 552.1175. Where a
record contains both public and excepted information, please redact the
excepted portion and release the remainder rather than withholding the record
entirely.

**Costs.** Please provide an itemized written estimate before incurring charges
if the total will exceed $40 (§ 552.2615). Charges should follow the Attorney
General's cost rules at 1 Tex. Admin. Code § 70.3. I agree to pay up to $100
without further authorization; above that, please contact me first.

If any part of this request is unclear, please contact me rather than denying
it — I am glad to narrow the years or file types if that reduces the burden.

Thank you for your assistance.

Sincerely,

Mark F. Young
mark.francis.young@gmail.com
