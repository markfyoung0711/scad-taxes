Where are Smith County property taxes hitting hardest?

Butterfly puts every Smith County property on one map, so you can see which neighborhoods were hit hardest since 2020, and why.

  • Look up any property. Search by owner, address or account number to see its market value, assessed value and tax bill year by year, and which taxing unit (county, school district, city or college) moved the bill.
  • See the whole county on a heat map. Red is where bills, values or rates rose most; blue is where they fell. Zoom in on any red patch and click a property to see its history.
  • Find the hot spots by name. A ranked report lists the subdivisions, neighborhoods, precincts, ZIP codes and streets hit hardest, with their main streets so you can place them. Click one to outline it on the map, or download the list.

The controls

What the map measures

Show Change over a range (how hard owners were hit) or Amount in one year (what they pay now).
Years Any start and end year from 2020 to 2026, or a single year.
Measure Tax bill, market (appraised) value, assessed value, or combined tax rate.

Which properties count

Property type Single-family homes, rural land, commercial, vacant lots and more.
Homesteads only Just owner-occupied homes.
Exclude new construction On by default. A lot that gained a house wasn't hit by a tax increase; it gained a house.

How the map looks

Smoothing From block-by-block detail (150 m) to broad regional trends (2.4 km).
Surface opacity, parcel dots, theme See the streets underneath, hide the dots, or switch to dark mode.

The region report

Group by Subdivision, appraisal neighborhood, voting precinct, ZIP code, or street.
Colors Show only red and orange regions (the default), or any mix of colors.
Minimum parcels Hide regions too small to call a trend.

Getting around

  • Hover a dot to see its account, its change and its region.
  • Click a dot, or lasso an area, to pin its regions at the top of the report and see each property's history.
  • Click a report row to zoom the map to that region, outlined in black.
  • Sort any report column, and download the report as a spreadsheet.

Figures come from the Smith County Appraisal District's published records. Butterfly is an independent tool, not an official county or appraisal district site; for official values, protests and deadlines, visit smithcad.org.
